# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue.js (requested by the user). Backend and hosting undecided.

## Users

Two audiences of equal weight:
- Current Zontes owners in Egypt who buy parts, oils and accessories, book maintenance, and follow their bike's service history.
- People looking to buy a used motorcycle who want to trust its condition before paying.

The home page must route each audience to its path quickly. The primary action on arrival is buying from the shop.

## Product Purpose

Online home of جراج أبو يوسف (Abu Yousef Garage), a Zontes-specialist workshop. It sells parts, oils and accessories, lists used bikes for sale that Abu Yousef has inspected, takes maintenance bookings, and gives members an account with their registered vehicles and full maintenance record. Success: shop orders, booked services, and serious enquiries on listed bikes.

## Positioning

A single specialist workshop, not a marketplace. Every used bike listed carries Abu Yousef's own inspection; members' maintenance history is recorded by the same hands that did the work.

## Operating Context

- Arabic-first (Egyptian dialect), RTL. Prices in EGP.
- Zontes model line (ZT125, ZT310, ZT350, ZT703 families); parts compatibility by model matters.
- Bookings are confirmed by WhatsApp/SMS; payment for service happens at the garage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Member registration and login (phone number, possibly WhatsApp code).
- Shop: catalog, cart, checkout, delivery or pickup at the garage.
- Bikes for sale with inspection report.
- Maintenance booking: vehicle → service → date/time → confirm.
- Member area: registered vehicles, odometer, next service, maintenance history, orders.
- Undecided: payment methods online, delivery coverage, number of products at launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: جراج أبو يوسف / Abu Yousef Garage. Tagline on the logo: "Let your Zontes Roar".
- The existing cartoon logo exists (design-concepts/logo.png) but the user chose to keep only the name; logo colors and cartoon style are NOT binding.
- STANDING PREFERENCE (2026-11-07): a conventional, familiar e-commerce layout (header search, category nav, banners, product grids, standard product/cart/checkout pages), executed clean and chic. No concept-driven or metaphor layouts.
- User rejected: loud/flashy treatments, gimmick concepts (instrument cluster, pegboard, comic, street signage), and palette-swaps of one layout. Wants chic, premium, calm, easy to use.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo file only. No product photos, bike photos, reviews, prices, or address supplied yet. All such content in mockups is placeholder and must be replaced.

## Product Principles

1. The shop comes first, but bike buyers must find their path within one glance.
2. Trust is shown, not claimed: inspection reports and service records are the proof.
3. Calm over loud: fewer elements, clear hierarchy, nothing decorative that slows a purchase.
4. Model compatibility is always visible when buying parts.
